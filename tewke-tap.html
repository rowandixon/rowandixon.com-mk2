Portfolio/Tewke Tap

Tewke Tap is the intelligent light switch at the centre of Tewke, the energy IoT company I helped co-found in late 2020 to develop the next generation of home automation products. By controlling all the energy in the home, and through the advanced sensors built into the devices, Tewke can save up to 30% of energy in the home.
