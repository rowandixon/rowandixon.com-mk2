Roupell L1

Reclaimed Copper Lamp

Portfolio/Roupell L1

A filament desk lamp constructed out of waste copper piping, with E27 filament bulbs for a retro-classic aesthetic. A floor button switch is integrated into a 4-metre braided power cable, positioned 1.2 metres from the lamp base to suit standard desk placement. Prototyped and assembled at home using a Dremel 3000 to cut and finish the copper piping, across Mk 1 and Mk 2 iterations.
