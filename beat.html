BEAT

Electro-Mechanical Heart

Portfolio/BEAT

BEAT is an interactive installation which takes the user's pulse via an optical heart rate sensor and then syncs a mechatronic copper heart with the user's own pulse. It drew from Victorian-era mechatronic devices as well as V-twin motorbike engines — the heart mechanism uses springs, cams, gears and pistons powered by a 12-volt motor. The cabinet interior uses thick black felt to minimise light and focus attention on the suspended copper heart; embedded LED lighting intensifies as the heart's speed increases to match the user's pulse, creating an atmosphere of excitement and energy.

Team projectRowan Dixon, Laerke Rasmussen, Hugo Hasted, William Pepera, Alfie Thompson
