Mountaineering

Leading small expeditions since 2016

Away from Tewke, mountaineering is where I go looking for a different kind of hard problem. What started as a self-led trip to summit Mont Blanc in 2016 grew into an attempt on Mt Elbrus in 2017, and two overland crossings of the Sahara in 2019 — each one its own lesson in logistics, weather, and how much can go wrong before you've even left.
