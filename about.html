About

Rowan Dixon

Designer · Engineer · DIY’er

I love physical products; from clicky buttons, to the cold touch of metal, I have always been fascinated by the way designers use different shapes, textures and mechanisms to augment a user’s experience. Since graduating in 2020 from Imperial in Design Engineering I have followed that passion to build Tewke, leading the product engineering and operations.

I love working with incredible people to bring the best possible products to market, and meeting different users and businesses that all culminate to make Tewke. Not only have I had the opportunity to be creative and play with emerging technologies but also work with fantastic people across different disciplines, such as software, graphic design and mechanical engineers.

Away from workI love to challenge myself, from running ultra-marathons to crossing the Sahara desert. I find the process of not being able to do something, and then working to achieve it incredibly rewarding.
