Tewke Tap

Physical AI for the home

Tewke is an consumer technology company I helped co-found in late 2020 developing the next generation of home automation devices that can control your whole home, from lighting and heating, to music and monitoring air quality, whilst also reducing energy bills by upto 30%. Tewke Tap is Tewke's flagship product, packing a 1080p OLED display, 7 environmental sensors including mm-wave radar.

RoleLeading the technical team — hardware, electronics and software — plus business operations and a consistent design language across Tewke OS, the mobile app, and the hardware itself.

Also from Tewke

Tap One

Tap One is the second device I oversaw at Tewke. It takes the core essence of what made Tewke Tap an award winning device, and brought it down to a more competitive price. It features the core sensors as Tap, but instead of an OLED display (and the high end CPU/GPU required to power such a display) it has a capacitive glass surface with an interactive LED light bar.
